# What is paediatric interventional radiology?

Paediatric interventional radiology (IR) uses image guidance — ultrasound, X-ray, or CT — to diagnose or treat children with small instruments instead of open surgery.

Common reasons a child may see an IR team include:

- Placing or removing a PICC line or other central venous access
- Taking a needle biopsy of an organ or lump
- Treating a vascular malformation with sclerotherapy or embolization
- Draining an abscess or fluid collection

Most IR procedures are done with sedation or general anaesthesia so the child stays still and comfortable. The IR doctor, nurses, and radiographers explain the plan to the family before the day of the procedure.

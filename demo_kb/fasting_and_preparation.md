# Fasting and preparation before an IR procedure

Children usually need an empty stomach before sedation or anaesthesia. Exact fasting times come from the hospital, not from this chatbot.

Typical guidance families hear (confirm with HKCH):

- Clear fluids may be allowed closer to the procedure than milk or food
- Breast milk and formula have different cut-off times from solid food
- Regular medicines are only given if the clinical team says so
- Bring identification, immunisation record, and any allergy information

On the day:

- Arrive at the time written on the admission letter
- A parent or guardian usually stays with the child until they go into the procedure room
- Remove jewellery and tell staff about loose teeth, recent illness, or new medicines

If the child is unwell (fever, cough, vomiting) before the appointment, call the IR nurse coordinator. The team may postpone the procedure for safety.

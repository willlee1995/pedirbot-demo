# Embolization and aftercare (educational overview)

Embolization is an IR procedure that blocks abnormal or bleeding blood vessels from inside the vessel, using a catheter and materials chosen by the IR doctor. In children it may be used for some vascular malformations or bleeding that has not settled with other treatment.

Before the procedure, the team discusses:

- Why embolization is being offered
- Sedation or anaesthesia
- Contrast dye, radiation, and possible risks such as pain, bruising, or (rarely) non-target vessel blockage
- What to expect in recovery

Aftercare families are commonly told (always follow the written HKCH leaflet):

- Rest for the period the team specifies
- Watch the puncture site for bleeding or swelling
- Give pain relief only as prescribed
- Seek urgent help for heavy bleeding, trouble breathing, sudden severe pain, or a child who becomes unusually drowsy or unresponsive

Call 999 or go to the nearest Accident and Emergency department for a true emergency. This page is educational and does not replace the consent discussion with the IR doctor.
